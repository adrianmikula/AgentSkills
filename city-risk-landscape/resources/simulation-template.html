Methodology & Data Sources

  • Attack probability (EXPONENTIAL): Base rate 38% from ASD Annual Cyber Threat Report 2024-25. Traditional security DECAYS: 50% effectiveness lost per year as AI attacks evolve—static defenses cannot adapt. AI-Powered security IMPROVES: 8% effectiveness gain per year as system learns. AI-era 1.35× compounding from Year 2.
  • Security Approach: None: No protection (2× recovery time). Traditional: Human-driven—MFA, backups, training. Year 1: 75% attack reduction. Year 5: Only 6% reduction as AI attacks bypass static defenses. AI-Powered: Automated defense—anomaly detection, automated response. Year 1: 94% reduction. Year 5: 97% reduction as AI adapts. Traditional security is becoming OBSOLETE—AI attacks evolve faster than human-driven defenses can adapt.
  • AI Business Tools (EXPONENTIAL): Yes/No toggle. AI businesses capture market share EXPONENTIALLY: Year 1 +25%, Year 2 +56%, Year 3 +95%, Year 4 +144%, Year 5 +205%. Staff cost savings immediate: 15% reduction. Attack surface increase: fixed +35%.
  • Reputational Damage (EXPONENTIAL): Breach harm compounds virally as bad news spreads. Initial hit: 12% revenue loss. Year 2: 14.4% (20% amplification). Year 3: 17.3%. Year 4: 20.7%. Year 5: 24.9%. Digital permanence + network effects create lasting damage that grows, not fades.
  • Survival model (with CASH FLOW CATASTROPHE): 20% baseline closure risk (VikingCloud 2025). Revenue-scaled: <$500k=35%, $500k-$2M=20%, >$2M=10%. Cash flow insolvency: Low-margin businesses have 1.5 months cash reserves vs 6+ months for high-margin. If breach cost exceeds available cash, immediate insolvency risk up to 80%. Profit margin determines both resilience multiplier AND cash buffer depth.
