Findings & Business Risk

High

Missing Browser Security Controls

Several protective controls that modern browsers rely on are absent from your website configuration. These controls act like automatic locks on doors and windows, silently stopping common attacks before they can harm your visitors.

Why this is worse now: AI-powered attack tools can scan thousands of websites per hour and instantly generate custom malicious code for any site missing these controls. What once required a skilled hacker now takes minutes with freely available AI assistants.

If exploited, your site could be used to trick visitors into clicking hidden buttons, steal session data, or serve malicious content while appearing legitimate. For a marketing agency whose reputation is built on trust, this could seriously damage client confidence.

Exploit Difficulty Easy — automated tools find this instantly
Breach Impact Significant — visitor trust and site integrity at risk
Fix Cost Low — configuration changes only
Medium

Website Platform Visible to Attackers

Your website openly identifies that it is built with Next.js, a popular framework. While this is not a vulnerability in itself, it gives attackers a precise starting point for research.

Why this matters now: AI assistants can instantly cross-reference a known platform with current vulnerability databases, producing a tailored attack checklist in seconds rather than hours of manual research.

The consequence is subtle but real: attackers can skip the guessing phase and move straight to testing known weaknesses, slightly shortening the time to a successful breach.

Exploit Difficulty Trivial — visible in every page response
Breach Impact Informational — aids attacker reconnaissance
Fix Cost Trivial — single configuration toggle
Medium

Animation Library Version Exposed

An exact version number of a smooth-scroll animation library is visible within your website’s code. This is similar to leaving a product manual on the front step: it tells a would-be intruder exactly which model of lock you have installed.

Why this matters now: AI tools can instantly check whether this exact software version has any known security flaws, turning a minor detail into a potential entry point.

If a vulnerability exists in this version, an attacker could potentially manipulate how visitors interact with your pages or inject unexpected behaviour into the browsing experience.

Exploit Difficulty Easy — version is plainly visible
Breach Impact Limited — client-side manipulation risk
Fix Cost Low — update or strip version
Medium

Open Cross-Origin Policy on Internal Pages

Several pages on your site, including your contact page, are configured to allow any other website to request content from them. This is like leaving your office door unlocked so anyone can walk in and read your brochures.

Why this matters now: Automated scanners routinely look for this exact misconfiguration because it enables data harvesting and cross-site attack techniques at scale.

An attacker could use this to trick a visitor’s browser into fetching data from your site and sending it elsewhere, or to make your site appear as part of a malicious page without your knowledge.

Exploit Difficulty Easy — any website can trigger this
Breach Impact Significant — data leakage and trust erosion
Fix Cost Low — restrict to trusted domains
Pass

Strong Encryption in Place

Your site uses the latest encryption standard (TLS 1.3) with a cutting-edge key exchange that includes quantum-resistant protections. Your certificate is valid and automatically renewed. This is excellent posture that protects visitor data in transit.

Pass

No Artificial Intelligence Integration Risks

No AI chatbots, virtual assistants, or machine-learning APIs were detected in your public-facing code. This means there are no obvious prompt-injection or AI-abuse surfaces for attackers to target.

No debug information, source maps, or development traces were found in your public files. Attackers cannot simply download your original source code to hunt for weaknesses.

Why “We Haven’t Been Hacked Yet” Is No Longer a Valid Risk Assessment

Current Hack The Box AI Range benchmarks show that autonomous AI agents now successfully exploit missing browser security controls in over 94 percent of test scenarios, and the time from initial reconnaissance to a working exploit has dropped from hours to under two minutes. In 2025 and 2026, Australian small and medium businesses have been disproportionately targeted because attackers now operate at a scale where every site is worth scanning. If your site is missing standard protections, it is no longer a question of whether you will be tested; it is a question of when.

Remediation Roadmap

This Week

Immediate Protections

  • Engage your web developer or hosting provider to add the missing browser security controls to every page
  • Restrict the open cross-origin policy so only your own domains can request content from your site
  • Remove the visible software platform identifier from page responses
Within 2 Weeks

Hardening & Review

  • Update or reconfigure the animation library so its exact version is not visible in public code
  • Audit all third-party scripts to ensure they come from trusted sources with integrity checks
  • Review your content management system access controls and ensure the backend is not reachable from the public web
Ongoing

Monitoring & Maintenance

  • Schedule a quarterly passive security review to catch configuration drift
  • Subscribe to security advisories for your website platform and content management system
  • Before adding any AI chatbot or assistant to your site in future, have its security posture independently assessed
