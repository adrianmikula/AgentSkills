# AI-Era Security Audit Report — Outreach Template

This resource contains the reusable outreach templates for the AI-Era Security Audit Report offering. It is loaded by `Skill.md` when the selected offering is "AI-Era Security Audit Report".

---

## Research Instructions (Executed Before Template Population)

Before populating either template, perform the following research steps:

1. **Search for recent breaches** in `{{COUNTRY}}` from 2025–2026, focusing on:
   - Victims in or near `{{CITY}}` or the same state/province/region.
   - Victims operating in `{{INDUSTRY}}` or an adjacent sector (e.g., hospitality → food & beverage, retail → e-commerce).
   - **Obscure, non-high-profile victims** — family-owned businesses, regional franchises, niche suppliers, small manufacturers, local service providers.

2. **Select 2–3 examples** that clearly illustrate:
   - The victim was not a household name or large enterprise.
   - The attack was likely automated or scalable (ransomware listing, mass WordPress compromise, supply-chain hit).
   - The compromise happened recently enough to feel current (2025–2026).

3. **Verify sources** are reputable: cyber-security news sites (BleepingComputer, CyberDaily, Comparitech), mainstream news, or government advisories.

4. **Localise spelling and phrasing** to match `{{COUNTRY}}`:
   - Australia / UK: "localise", "programme", "organisation", "centre", "analyse", "behaviour", "colour", "favour", "neighbour".
   - United States: "localize", "program", "organization", "center", "analyze", "behavior", "color", "favor", "neighbor".

5. **Adjust tone for `{{BUSINESS_SIZE}}`**:
   - **Micro:** Very personal, direct, empathetic. Acknowledge limited IT resources. Shorter examples.
   - **Small:** Friendly peer-to-peer. Acknowledge they have a website or online presence but maybe no dedicated security person.
   - **Medium:** Professional but approachable. Assume some IT awareness but highlight gaps an AI audit can quickly surface.
