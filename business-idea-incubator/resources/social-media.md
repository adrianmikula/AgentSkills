# Social Media Presence

This resource covers **Social Media Presence** — building and maintaining an authentic social media footprint that drives awareness, trust, and revenue for your business.

## Key Frameworks

- **Content Pillars** — 3-5 core themes that define your brand voice and content strategy
- **Platform Strategy** — Where your audience actually is (not where it's trendy)
- **Engagement Loop** — Post → Engage → Respond → Repeat (build community, not just audience)
- **Social Proof Stack** — Testimonials, case studies, user-generated content, influencer validation
- **Content Batching** — Produce a week/month of content in one focused session

## Common Pitfalls

- **Being everywhere** — Posting on 6 platforms poorly vs. 2 platforms well
- **Only promotional content** — 80/20 rule: 80% value, 20% promotion
- **Ignoring analytics** — Posting without knowing what resonates
- **Inconsistent voice** — Different tone on every platform confuses your audience
- **Buying followers** — Vanity metrics don't convert to customers
