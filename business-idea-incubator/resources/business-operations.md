# Business Operations

This resource covers **Business Operations** — the systems, processes, and infrastructure needed to run a business efficiently as it grows.

## Key Frameworks

- **OKRs** — Objectives and Key Results for aligning team and measuring progress
- **EOS (Entrepreneurial Operating System)** — Vision, People, Data, Issues, Process, Traction
- **Lean Startup** — Build-Measure-Learn loop, validated learning, innovation accounting
- **Value Stream Mapping** — Identify waste in processes and optimize flow
- **RACI Matrix** — Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, Informed (clarify roles)

## Common Pitfalls

- **Process too early** — Adding bureaucracy before you have product-market fit
- **No metrics dashboard** — Flying blind without real-time data on what matters
- **Hiring for gaps instead of strengths** — Fixing weaknesses with headcount instead of systems
- **Ignoring cash flow** — Profit ≠ cash in bank; timing matters
- **Founder as bottleneck** — Everything goes through you; the business can't run without you
