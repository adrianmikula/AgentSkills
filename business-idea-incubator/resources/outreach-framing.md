# Outreach & Offer Framing

This resource covers **Outreach & Offer Framing** — how to structure your product/service offer, price it, and communicate value to potential customers through direct outreach and sales conversations.

## Key Frameworks

- **Value Ladder** — Lead magnet → Tripwire → Core Offer → Profit Maximizer
- **Grand Slam Offer** (Alex Hormozi) — Make your offer so good people feel stupid saying no
- **PAS / BBB / SB** — Copywriting frameworks for outreach messages
- **SPIN Selling** — Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff (consultative selling)
- **The 5 Types of Offers** — Intro offer, Core offer, Scaling offer, Community, Event
- **Pricing Psychology** — Charm pricing, anchoring, decoy effect, prestige pricing

## Common Pitfalls

- **Weeks of free consulting** — Giving away the solution in the first call
- **Price anchoring too low** — Starting cheap makes it hard to raise prices later
- **Feature dumping** — Listing features instead of painting the outcome
- **No clear next step** — Ending conversations without a defined action
- **Ignoring the "who"** — Outreach templates fail when not personalized to the recipient

## Output Templates

### Offer Structure Template

```
# Offer Design: {{IDEA_NAME}}

## Core Offer
- **Product/Service:** [What you sell]
- **Outcome:** [What customer gets]
- **Price:** $[amount] / [model]
- **Delivery:** [How it's delivered]

## Value Stack
| Component | Value (if sold separately) |
|-----------|---------------------------|
| [Main deliverable] | $[amount] |
| [Bonus 1] | $[amount] |
| [Bonus 2] | $[amount] |
| **Total Value** | **$[amount]** |
| **Your Price** | **$[amount]** |

## Pricing Rationale
- **Model:** [One-time / Subscription / Usage-based / Tiered]
- **Anchor:** [What price are you anchoring against?]
- **Psychological trigger:** [Charm / Prestige / Value]

## Outreach Angle
- **Opening:** [Hook in 1 sentence]
- **Pain:** [Problem you solve]
- **Proof:** [Social proof or data point]
- **Offer:** [What you're proposing]
- **CTA:** [Clear next step]
```

### Cold Outreach Template

```
Subject: [Specific observation about their business]

Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific observation about their company/role].
[Empathize with a pain point this creates].

I've been working with [similar companies] on [outcome],
and [brief proof point or case study].

Would you be open to a 15-minute chat this week to explore
if this could help [their company]?

Best,
[Your name]
```
