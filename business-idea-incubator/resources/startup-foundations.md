# Startup Foundations

This resource covers **Startup Foundations** — the legal, structural, and philosophical underpinnings of launching a startup, including entity formation, equity, cofounder relationships, and early governance.

## Key Frameworks

- **Founder-Market Fit** — Are you the right person to build this? Do you have unique insight, access, or passion?
- **Equity Splits** — Dynamic equity models, vesting schedules, and avoiding founder disputes
- **Legal Structures** — Pty Ltd, LLC, Corporation, sole trader: when to use what
- **Founder Agreements** — Roles, responsibilities, vesting, IP assignment, and exit clauses
- **Runway Planning** — How much capital you need, burn rate, and when to raise

## Common Pitfalls

- **Equal splits without vesting** — 50/50 splits with no vesting mean a founder who leaves early keeps half the company
- **No founder agreement** — Verbal agreements create expensive legal disputes later
- **Premature scaling** — Spending on marketing before proving unit economics
- **Ignoring cap table hygiene** — Messy equity ownership scares investors
- **Solo founder fatigue** — Underestimating the psychological and logistical burden of going alone

### Cofounder Agreement Checklist

- [ ] Roles and responsibilities defined
- [ ] Equity split agreed with vesting (4-year standard, 1-year cliff)
- [ ] IP assignment clause included
- [ ] Decision-making process documented
- [ ] Exit / founder departure terms agreed
- [ ] Dispute resolution mechanism in place
